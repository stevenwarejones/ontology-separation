## Comparison outcome

The immediate follow-up should test whether a specified intervention changes unconditioned outcome probabilities in the way two declared models predict. It should first close the finite-window/instrument correspondence, because an exact algebraic separator cannot repair an inaccurate apparatus model. A phase scan has established precedents; no novelty claim is made here. Its contribution would be a transparent connection between intervention, calibration, data and the universe classes represented in this repository.

The stronger claim—excluding a justified noncontextual ontological class—requires the package-2 equivalence and disturbance analysis. The still different claim—operational influence outside a light cone or into the past—requires package 1's spacetime and causal controls. None is inferred from the others.

## Outstanding searches with closure conditions

Before apparatus selection, review the closest regional-phase experiment and its loss/calibration supplement in full. Before a contextuality implementation, audit the chosen theorem's proof and instrument construction, its experimental predecessors, and finite-statistics treatment of operational equivalences. Before interpreting raw Wen results, refresh author versions, corrections and released code and inspect the actual workbook schemas. These are targeted dependencies, not a declaration that all quantum-foundations literature has been exhausted. Record positive and adverse findings in `literature-search-log.md` and add newly material questions to the register.
