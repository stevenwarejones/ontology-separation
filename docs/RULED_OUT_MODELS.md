# Ruled-out law packages

Retaining a failed model gives a useful reference prediction. Experimental rejection
is statistical and conditional on test assumptions; mathematical exclusion of a
specified probability table is different.

| Included package | Checked prediction | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| Predetermined local responses A(x,λ), B(y,λ) and independent preparation | S ≤ 2; a concrete constant world attains 2 | Bell-local null rejected statistically under the experiment's assumptions |
| Stochastic screening-off, conditional parameter independence, independent preparation | S ≤ 2 | Same Bell-local experimental target; not an independent discovery |
| A common distribution of local counterfactual assignments | S ≤ 2 | Equivalent Bell-local correlation class in the finite setting |
| Readable fixed friend records, conditional locality, independent preparation | G ≤ 6 | LF photonic-proxy evidence; not a general refutation for cognitive observers |

`ClassicalWorld.Model Λ` supplies a normalized preparation distribution and local
response functions. The bounds are derived from that law package.
`cannot_reproduce_singlet` proves incompatibility with our calculated singlet table.
`constantWorld` demonstrates that the class is nonempty. Countermodels show how
relaxing response locality or preparation independence can evade the Bell ceiling,
without asserting those alternatives describe nature.

[Hensen et al. (2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.05949) report an event-ready Bell
test with 245 trials, S=2.42±0.20 and a local-realist null-test p-value of 0.039,
allowing memory in the devices. This is statistical rejection subject to the paper's
experimental and setting-choice assumptions. The p-value is not an ontology's
probability, and the error bar is not imported as a proved confidence bound. Our
finite-response theorem does not reimplement the memory-robust statistical analysis;
no raw-data reanalysis is included.

[Bong et al. (2020), published version](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.05607v4) demonstrate
a proof-of-principle LF violation using a photon's path as an observer proxy. This
does not establish that general absolute-record models of conscious observers have
been experimentally falsified. The repository's exact LF value is a calculated
quantum witness, not the published measurement; no experimental LF score or p-value
is fabricated here.

Realism alone, locality alone, independence alone, quantum theory, Bohmian mechanics,
many-worlds, and broad collapse-model families are not labeled false by name. An
excluded conjunction can have several different surviving alternatives.
