BellStudy: two-qubit Bell experiment

Exact CHSH E00+E01+E10-E11 from normalized two-qubit Born probabilities (false=+1, true=-1). Real amplitudes and local projective measurements. Gates prepare the state before separated setting choices. Only explicit exposure steps vary with the row's local Z-dephasing law; p=1 is full dephasing. The Bell-local ceiling is 2; a larger calculated value excludes that class for this mathematical behavior, not from laboratory data.
