Published experimental evidence

Bong et al. (2020): Local Friendliness

Reports a proof-of-principle LF-inequality violation using a photon's path as the observer proxy.

Relevant to the stated physical proxy. Does not establish experimental control of a cognitive observer or rule out observer-scale ontologies in general.

Literature record only; no numerical LF dataset imported or statistical analysis reverified here. Our exact singlet score is a calculation, not this measurement.

Remaining ambition: Ideal reversible friend model; no claim that a qubit is a cognitive observer.
